IMPORTANT:
BCS MUST NOT be calculated from:
- actual body weight
- body length
- heart girth
- withers height
- YOLO features
- model predictions

The BCS value must come from an independent reference assessment.


GENERAL SCALE

1.00
Very severely underconditioned.
Major skeletal landmarks are extremely prominent.

2.00
Clearly thin.
Backbone, ribs and pelvic landmarks are prominent.
Limited fat covering.

2.50
Moderately thin.
Some skeletal landmarks remain obvious,
but there is more tissue covering than at BCS 2.

2.75
Slightly thin.
Backbone may remain visible.
Hooks/pins are relatively angular.

3.00
Good/average condition.
Backbone and pelvic landmarks are rounded.
Ribs are reasonably covered.
No obvious deep hollowing around the tailhead.

3.25
Slightly fleshy.
More tissue covering over the backbone and pelvic region.
Landmarks remain identifiable but are increasingly rounded.

3.50
Good-to-fleshy condition.
More substantial fat covering.
Pelvic landmarks remain identifiable.

3.75
Fleshy.
Increasing fat deposition around rump/tailhead.
Landmarks become less prominent.

4.00
Overconditioned.
Backbone and ribs are difficult to distinguish.
Hooks and pins are smooth and well covered.

4.50
Very overconditioned.
Strong fat covering.
Pelvic and rump landmarks are difficult to distinguish.

5.00
Extremely overconditioned.
Major skeletal landmarks are heavily obscured by fat.


ASSESSMENT RULES
================

1. Examine the complete cow image.

2. Concentrate on visible body-condition landmarks,
   especially the loin, rump/pelvic region and tailhead.

3. Do NOT use body weight to decide the score.

4. Do NOT use the computer-vision measurements to decide
   the score.

5. If the image does not provide enough visual information,
   mark the assessment as REVIEW rather than guessing.

6. Use 0.25 increments only.

7. The same scoring protocol should be used for every cow.

8. Ideally, the same trained assessor should score the complete
   dataset.

9. If multiple assessors are available, preserve each assessor's
   identity so inter-rater agreement can later be measured.

QUALITY CONTROL
===============

Before model training:

- Every training cow must have a valid BCS.
- BCS must be between 1.00 and 5.00.
- BCS must be a multiple of 0.25.
- No duplicate cow IDs.
- No duplicate image IDs.
- Images marked REVIEW should not enter training.
- BCS labels must be independent of the ML features.
